How the 30 minutes will probably go

Manager interviews usually mix your story, one or two project deep dives, a design discussion, and time for your questions. Use this to pace yourself.

  1. 0–3 min

    Introductions and your background

    Deliver the 90-second story. End by pointing toward the J&J project so they ask about it.

  2. 3–13 min

    Deep dive on your current project

    Architecture, hallucination controls, HITL, the turnaround metric. Draw the system if there's a whiteboard or shared screen.

  3. 13–20 min

    Technical breadth

    RAG, agents versus workflows, MCP, GCP choices, testing and CI/CD. Short answers; let them pick what to dig into.

  4. 20–26 min

    Design or scenario question

    Clarify requirements, sketch components, name trade-offs, mention evaluation and cost.

  5. 26–30 min

    Your questions

    Ask two thoughtful questions. Thank them and restate your interest in one sentence.

Do

  • Lead with the answer, then give detail.
  • Say "I" for your work and "we" for the team's, and be clear which is which.
  • Name trade-offs: what you chose, what you gave up.
  • Pause and ask "want me to go deeper on any part?"
  • Say "I haven't done that, but here's how I'd approach it" when true.

Avoid

  • Answers over two minutes without checking in.
  • Claiming you ran RLHF training when it was a feedback loop.
  • Sharing confidential J&J details or client names beyond your resume.
  • Framework lists without the reason you chose each.
  • Speaking negatively about a past employer.
